## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Practices and games that can't be placed are listed as TIME TBD, in the master export too** (item E of `docs/changes/2026-10-27-phase-d-scheduling.md`). A practice a team is still short of, and a game the scheduler could not place, used to be in no file: the master CSV and XLSX held only what had a time, so a team's missing practice or game was a silence. Now **Generate CSVs** lists each as a row with **Start `TIME TBD`**, **Field `LOCATION TBD`**, the End blank and the reason in Notes: a team two practices short gets two rows ("Practice not scheduled (1 of 2 missing): no slot was free for it."), an unplaced game gets a home row and an away row with the sides it was given. The Exports page says how many ("2 practices and 1 game not scheduled yet are in the files as TIME TBD, with the reason.") and lists them under the files; the team files carry the same rows after the team's dated ones. A division at 0 practices a week lists nothing. The list is read from the database when you generate; if it cannot be read the files are not made and the page says why, rather than leaving the rows out. It is kept in its own table, `schedule_tbd`, and not as rows in the practice and game schedules: a row with no slot or dates there would read as a held practice to the lock check, the writer's prune, "teams without a practice" and the metrics (the owner allowed a separate table only if that broke the schedule, and it does). The practice save rebuilds a season's practice list **from the roster** and each division's count (never from what the page sent), counting a series that has ended as not held; the game save records the run's unscheduled games, each checked, and a save with no such list (a hand edit, a rollback) leaves the list alone. The published-baseline check numbers the TIME TBD rows of one team (1, 2, ...), so "2 TIME TBD then, 1 now" is a row removed instead of an ambiguity, and a TIME TBD Start no longer reads as a second time vocabulary or as an unreadable time. Migration `20261107010000` (the table with admin-only RLS, `get_schedule_tbd(season)`, `persist_practice_schedule` and `persist_game_schedule` replaced behind their md5, `admin_delete_team` counting `schedule_tbd_deleted`; revert and smoke in `docs/sql/`, pgTAP `supabase/tests/schedule_tbd.sql`). `tests/outputGeneration.test.js`, `tests/scheduleTbd.test.js`, `tests/publicationBaseline.test.js`, `tests/outputGenerationTbd.test.jsx`, `tests/gameUnscheduledRecorded.test.jsx`, `tests/mockScheduleTbd.test.js`, `tests/e2e/features/schedule_exports.feature`.

- **Practices per division, on different days; past sunset is flagged, not refused** (PR B of `docs/changes/2026-10-27-phase-d-scheduling.md`). A division now says how many practices a week each of its teams gets (0 to 7, one until you change it) and, optionally, how long each is (15 to 240 minutes, or any slot length): **Practices per week** and **Practice length (minutes)** on the Teams page, saved with the division's rules. The auto-scheduler gives every team that many, **each on a different weekday**, in a slot of exactly the length when one is set. A team given 1 of its 2 practices no longer vanishes from the run report: the Auto-Scheduler panel lists "Hawks: 1 of 2 practices (the division's slots fall on fewer weekdays than its practices a week)", and a length no slot matches says so ("no practice slot is exactly as long as the division's practice"). Lowering a count below the practices a team already holds keeps those practices and says so. A division at one a week, which is every division today, is scheduled exactly as before. **Past sunset is flagged, never blocked, for every league:** the auto-scheduler used to cut a placement at its first date past sunset and the repair refused such a re-home; both now keep the placement whole and show a warning with the date and by how many minutes it runs over. A sunset that cannot be worked out is still never read as daylight. The practice writer (`persist_practice_schedule`) now refuses a save whose new auto-scheduled rows break a slot's capacity, a reservation, a division's practice length, the different-weekday rule or the weekly count (counted per Monday-to-Sunday week, so a Monday and a Wednesday row of one week are two). A saved practice on a slot outside the run still holds its team's weekday, so a team is not given a second Tuesday; if such a practice cannot be dated the run is refused naming the team. A division at 0 practices a week is no shortfall: its teams are left out of the run's coverage, report and "left without a practice" list. A recommendation that runs past sunset shows its flag in the repair table and in both confirmation dialogs. Migration `20261105010000` (columns, the writer, `admin_upsert_division_settings` gaining `p_practices_per_week` and `p_practice_minutes`, and the helper `practice_new_auto_row_refusals`; revert and smoke in `docs/sql/`, pgTAP `supabase/tests/practices_per_division.sql` and `practice_writer_v3.sql`). TIME TBD rows in the master export (plan item E) are not in this PR: see the plan's build notes. `tests/autoSchedulerPracticeUnits.test.js`, `tests/practiceUnplacedReasons.test.js`, `tests/practiceLines.test.js`, `tests/autoSchedulerDaylight.test.js`, `tests/practiceRepairDaylight.test.js`, `tests/e2e/features/practices_per_division.feature`.

- **Home and away for games are balanced, a saved game keeps its sides, and a lopsided team is flagged** (PR A of `docs/changes/2026-10-27-phase-d-scheduling.md`). The game scheduler used to make the team with the smaller id the home team in every game it played, so one team hosted all of its games. Generating a schedule now shares hosting out: no team hosts more than one game more than it visits, in a division of an odd size every team hosts exactly half of its games, and no team plays more than two home or two away games in a row. Nothing else moves: every game keeps its week, slot and field. **A game you have saved keeps its home and away sides** when you generate again, even where the new rule would have flipped it, so a division saved before this change stays as it is. The game page's conflict list gains a **Home/Away** warning (advisory, nothing is blocked) for any team whose placed games are more than one apart, with the count and any games left unscheduled. No migration. `tests/gameScheduling.test.js`, `tests/gameMetrics.test.js`, `tests/gameSchedulingKeepSides.test.jsx`.

- **Only league admins sign in: no coach role, coach home or coach screens; admins can belong to several organizations** (owner decision of 2026-10-27, F1 to F7 of `docs/changes/2026-10-27-admin-only.md`; the server change is the next PR). Invites and Members & Roles offer Admin and Staff only. The coach home, My Team, the coach's schedule card, division list and permits card, the practice-preference request screen, role preview ("Preview as Coach") and the link-to-coach-record call on sign-in are gone. `/permits` is now the admins' **Field Permits** page under Scheduling (open or download every permit; upload stays in Fields & Venues). A redeemed invite now lands you in the organization you joined, and the password change and the Account and Season settings audit rows name the organization you are working in (they used the profile's, which is empty). A team's calendar link is an admin's to rotate and hand out.
- **The coach import links each coach to their children, and only such a link anchors a team** (owner decisions of 2026-10-27; S7 and S8 of `docs/changes/2026-10-27-admin-only.md`). A coach file can carry an optional **Child Registration ID** column (one or more player registration ids, separated by `;` or `,`). Import the players first: each id that matches a player of the organization becomes a link from the coach to that child, in the order the file lists them; an id that matches nobody is listed on the import screen, writes nothing and never stops the import; a re-import replaces the coach's links, and a rollback puts them back. A coach an import row names (`coach_email`, `coach_id`) no longer anchors a team on the Teams page: only these links do (decision 8a of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-b-teaming.md`), so the overview's recommended team count no longer counts head coaches; generation still makes a team for each linked coach whose child is eligible, and only a coach who is active or pending confirmation anchors one (the rule `admin_assign_team_coach` applies): an inactive coach's links stay on file, so re-activating the coach restores the anchor, but their child is placed like any other player until then. Rolling an import back no longer fails when a coach it covered has been deleted since. The template gains the column and the import screen says players come first. The same email in two organizations is now two coaches, one per organization, stored lower-case (`UNIQUE (organization_id, email)`). Contract: `docs/architecture/gotsport-import-contract.md`.
- **Coaches see the permits for their fields** (C3 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-c-permits.md`). A coach's home has a **Field permits** card beside their schedule, and the coach nav has **Field Permits** (`/permits`): the permits for the fields where a team they coach has a game or a practice, each with its number (text, leading zeros kept), issuer, dates, the fields it covers, and **Open** and **Download** buttons (at least 44 px, for a phone at the field) that make a two-minute signed link when tapped. The card says in words what needs attention ("Needs attention: 1 expired, 1 with no file, 1 expiring within 30 days"), says the permits are current when they are, and says plainly, not as an error, when no permit covers the coach's fields. **A permit blocks nothing**: an expired permit, or one with no file, is shown as it is. A member whose role the league no longer has (a former parent) is sent home from the page. No migration: the server's read was narrowed to the coach's fields in C1. `tests/coachPermitsPage.test.jsx`, `tests/coachPermitsCard.test.jsx`, `tests/coachHomePermits.test.jsx`, `tests/e2e/features/field_permits_coach.feature`, a real-backend scenario (`real_backend_permits.feature`, written, not yet run: no local stack was available).
- **Field and venue permits: record, upload, warn** (C1 and C2 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-c-permits.md`). Field Management has a **Permits** section: an admin records who issued a permit, its number (kept as text, leading zeros and all), the days it is valid, notes, and the venues and fields it covers, and attaches the document (a PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 10 MB; replaced, opened or downloaded from the list, deleted after a confirmation). Each permit says how long it has in words ("Expires in 12 days", "Expired 3 days ago") beside an icon; each field's card says "No current permit" or when its permit expires, and the admin home's Needs attention lists the fields with no current permit, the permits expiring within 30 days and the expired ones. **A permit never blocks anything:** no import, scheduling run, publish or export looks at one. Deleting a field names the permits it unlinks (the permit stays on record). In the database: `facility_permits` and `facility_permit_scopes` (read-only to clients), five audited RPCs, a private `facility-permits` bucket, and a coach read already narrowed to the fields where a team they coach plays or practises (the coach's own page is C3). Migration `20261102010000`, with revert and smoke; pgTAP `facility_permits.sql` and `storage_facility_permits_bucket.sql`.
- **Programs: optional groups of divisions** (E2 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-e-seasons.md`). Settings, Season & Calendar has a **Programs** section: a program groups divisions within a season (Junior: U7 and U8, boys and girls). An admin creates, renames, deletes and merges programs, and puts a division in a program, moves it to another or takes it out; a delete or a merge names the divisions that move before it happens, and a completed season's programs cannot be changed. Every change is one audited call (`program.upserted`, `division.program_changed`, `program.deleted`, `program.merged`), made under the organization's season lock. **Programs are optional**: a league that runs on divisions alone is never asked for one, every season has a default program shown as "No program" that holds every division in none of its own, every import and every division writer puts a new division there without knowing programs exist, and the Teams page lists the divisions under program headings only once a program of the league's own holds one (a league without one sees the flat list it always had). The demo league has its programs (Minis, Micro, Junior, Select) from the fixture's registration plans. The Teams page, its settings panel and the Coaches page now say "division" where they said "program" for what is a division. Migration `20261101020000` (`programs`, `divisions.program_id` refused to a plain client UPDATE by a trigger so the program calls stay its only writer, with a composite reference so a division cannot sit in another season's program, the two default-program triggers, the four writers, the demo start replaced); revert and smoke in `docs/sql/`, pgTAP `supabase/tests/programs.sql`; `tests/programs.test.js` (the mock twin), `tests/programsSection.test.jsx`, `tests/e2e/features/programs.feature`.
- **One active season per organization, and imports that carry their season** (E1 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-e-seasons.md`). The database now holds at most one `active` season per organization and season names unique per organization; a season's status is always set (a new one is a draft) and no client writes the table directly. Every reader of "the current season" now means the active one and never the newest: the app (a stored choice is the season's id, in its own setting, and a draft is shown only to an admin or staff member who chose it; the season switcher lists the active season first, marks the others' status and lists drafts to admins only), the Edge Functions that read a season's clock and the natural-age count, and the importers. An import adds to the season its job was made for (the active one, fixed when the job is made), and only the active season takes an import: a draft or completed season is refused (22023, `season_not_active` or `season_completed`) because a player belongs to the organization and an import into another season would move a live-season player; per-season enrolment, which lets a draft season take imports, is E3's. A completed season takes no division either. Nothing changes for a league with one active season, which is every league today: no draft can be created until Phase E3. Migration `20261101010000` (revert and smoke in `docs/sql/`, pgTAP `supabase/tests/one_active_season.sql`); `create_import_job` gains a trailing `p_season_settings_id` (the 3-argument call still works), and `finalize_import_job`, `finalize_field_import_job`, `upsert_division_for_import` and `admin_upsert_division_settings` are replaced. `tests/organizationContextSeasonDefault.test.jsx`, `tests/importSeason.test.js`.
- **Try the demo** (D4 and D5 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-demo-mode.md`). On a deployment that sets `VITE_DEMO_ENABLED=true`, the sign-in page offers **Try the demo**: a visitor with no account is signed in anonymously and lands in a private "Demo league" (D3) with a timer in the header, "Demo league · 22:14 left", on every page and a **Leave demo** button; a screen reader hears two polite announcements, at 5 minutes and at 1 minute left. At zero the visitor is signed out to a "Your demo has ended" page with **Start again** and **Create an account**. In a demo a player's name, gender and buddy request, and every coach record, are read-only, and a player can be neither added nor deleted (where players play and how they are rated can change); trying to import a file opens a notice, with a link to the self-hosting guide, instead of the file picker, and uploading an export is hidden. An anonymous session is never sent to organization creation. Cloudflare Turnstile guards sign-in, sign-up, password reset and the demo's entry when `VITE_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` is set (the CSP allows `challenges.cloudflare.com` for its script and frame). Error reports from a demo carry a `demo` tag. New optional variables `VITE_DEMO_ENABLED`, `VITE_OFFICIAL_SITE_URL` and `VITE_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY`. - **Practice-only divisions and practice slots kept for one division** (D2 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-demo-mode.md`). A division can now practise without playing games: untick **Plays games** in its rules on the Teams page, and the game scheduler builds it no fixtures and lists it under **Practice only** on the Game Scheduling page instead of counting its matchups as unscheduled; its teams still get practices. A practice slot can be kept for one division: choose the division on each slot in Field Management (**Practice slot divisions**), or fill the fields CSV's new optional **Division** column. A slot kept for a division is used only by that division's teams, and a division with slots of its own practises only in those, in the auto-scheduler and the core scheduler alike; a team left with no slot open to it is listed with its reason. The fields import now refuses a practice slot that overlaps another (back-to-back slots are allowed) on the same pitch or half on the same weekday in the same dates (two halves of one field may run at once), and a Division the season does not have; each refused row is reported with its reason and writes nothing. Migration `20261029010000` adds `divisions.plays_games` and `practice_slots.division_id` (a division of the slot's own organization), `admin_set_practice_slot_division` (admins only, audited as `practice_slot.division_set`) and the internal clash rule, and replaces the field import, its rollback (which now puts a slot's division back) and `admin_upsert_division_settings` (now taking `p_plays_games`). Game rows in the fields CSV read their division as before. Nothing changes for a league that sets neither: every division plays and every slot is open. Revert and smoke in `docs/sql/`, pgTAP in `supabase/tests/practice_only_divisions.sql`; `tests/practiceOnlyDivisions.test.js`, `tests/autoSchedulerReservations.test.js`, `tests/gameSchedulingPracticeOnly.test.jsx`, `tests/e2e/features/practice_only_divisions.feature`. - **Schedule downloads as CSV, Excel and a calendar file, for coaches and admins** (owner decision of 2026-10-22, decision 6; R7 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-admin-focus.md`). Every team page's schedule now downloads as CSV, as XLSX and as a `.ics` calendar: an admin's per-team export, and a coach's from My Team. A coach's home downloads the whole season of every team they coach in one CSV or XLSX, and each team's calendar. Every event the schedule shows is in the files, one row each: a TIME TBD practice, a game whose slot has no time and a game with no slot are listed with their status and the same reason the subscribed calendar gives. Times are the season's wall clock with its timezone beside them; no file carries an email, phone or any contact column. The calendar file is rendered by the subscribed feed's own code from the rows the page read. The Exports page's master schedule gains a **Download Master XLSX** (the CSV's rows, `Coach Emails` included, admin-only), and now says how many run assignments with no time the master files leave out. New dependency: `write-excel-file` 4.1.1 (MIT, one dependency, `fflate`), loaded only when an XLSX is asked for (19.10 KB gz); `xlsx` (two open high advisories, fixed only off the registry) and `exceljs` (no stable release since 2023-10, nine dependencies built on Node streams) were rejected. `tests/teamScheduleExport.test.js`, `tests/scheduleExportsUi.test.jsx`, `tests/outputGenerationXlsx.test.jsx`, `tests/e2e/features/schedule_exports.feature`
- **The drift check sees views** (`supabase/catalog-snapshot.json`, `scripts/dbharness/catalog_snapshot.sql`). Production lacked two views every fresh build has, and the check, which compared functions, triggers and policies, could not see it. The snapshot now lists each `public` view's name, kind, options and typed columns, so the harness fails a migration that adds, drops or reshapes a view without updating the snapshot, and the operator query against production names a view either side lacks. Run against production on 2026-10-24, it matches everywhere except two views no code reads, `coach_team_map` and `view_facility_usage`, which production never had (`docs/operations/schema-drift-2026-10.md`, section 7).
- **Buddy requests by name reach team generation** (Phase B1 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-b-teaming.md`). The Teams page now hands each player's buddy request, buddy code and registration id to the teaming pipeline (only while the Buddy Requests feature is on), with every imported player, so "not found" and "in another division" are told apart. A request written as a name, the GotSport form, is matched exactly after normalising accents, case and punctuation, on first and last name, preferred and last name, or "Last, First"; two players of one name are settled only when one of them names the requester back; a near miss is never linked. Before, a name never matched, and ids and codes were dropped before the pipeline. Two buddies who each anchor a different head coach's team are now placed with their own coaches and reported (`buddy-coach-conflict`) instead of aborting the run. The staged review shows a **Buddy requests** list: pairs and whether they landed together, one-way requests, and each request that matched nobody, with the reason and the candidates to correct it against; it is stored with the run. A new league setting, **Buddy rule** (feature settings), is `Mutual requests only` by default; `One-way requests, best effort` also keeps a one-way request together where the roster allows, when the named player listed no buddy of their own. Programs with name requests will produce different teams on their next fresh run; a re-run keeps preserved players where they are.

### Changed

- **The server admits league admins only** (owner decisions of 2026-10-27; S1 to S4 of `docs/changes/2026-10-27-admin-only.md`; migrations `20261104010000_admin_only.sql` and `20261104020000_drop_coach_user_link.sql`). The `coach` role is gone from the member and invite checks, and every read that had a coach arm (coaches, assignments, calendar tokens, coach practice preferences, lighting overrides, permits and their files, the team roster and staff) is an admin's. A coach is data an admin manages: the email stays as the import's key and the admins' contact, and `coaches.user_id` and `profile_id` are dropped (production held one link, the admin's own, and none; the account, its profile and its membership are untouched). The calendar subscription links stay, admin-only. Each replaced function is guarded by the md5 of the body this build holds, so a deploy over a body it has not seen stops with nothing changed. Reverts restore the structure, not the dropped link (`docs/sql/20261104010000_revert.sql`, `20261104020000_revert.sql`).
- **Team generation leaves out waitlisted players and players who cannot be placed from the league-wide averages; coaches and their children are flagged on the review; Spread Coaching Leads needs Coaching Interest; Team Builder pairs co-ed buddies** (owner decisions of 2026-10-27 on the Phase B questions; "Owner decisions (2026-10-27)" in `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-b-teaming.md`). The Teams page now holds a **waitlisted** player (the stored status, or the file's waitlist column while the Waitlist feature is on) out of every team, listed under "Not placed" with the reason; the averages a team-balancing score compares each player to are taken over the players who can be placed, so neither a waitlisted nor a not-eligible player skews them. A **coach whose only child is not placed** is named in "Not placed" with "has no team to anchor", and a **coach with more than one child in a division** is listed with the team each child is on (nothing pairs them: the admin decides). **Spread Coaching Leads** is shown but disabled, with the reason, while Coaching Interest is off; its setting is kept. In **Team Builder**, buddies pair within a stored division (a single co-ed division like U4 pairs a boy and a girl); on a board that folds the boys' and girls' divisions, a pair split across the two is shown as "in different divisions, so not paired", and auto-balance deals each division to its own teams so every save is accepted. A waitlisted coach's child no longer sanctions a younger buddy's play-up. No migration.
- **The practice auto-scheduler stops at the server's time limit and returns its best schedule** (owner decision of 2026-10-27). A Supabase Edge Function request gets 2 seconds of CPU, and a large league (about 128 teams) could run past it and be cut off with nothing returned. The search now stops after about 1.5 seconds of the request (or the run's own limit, if shorter), the run completes with the best schedule found, and the result says `cpu-cap` as its termination reason; the Auto-Scheduler panel then reads "Stopped at the server's time limit. This is the best schedule found in that time; check any unassigned teams before you apply it." A small league finishes by its own limit and gets the schedule it always did. Re-running does not search further: the search restarts from the same seed.
- **The coach view: a coach's own schedule and division on their home; scheduling, score entry, standings and lighting are an admin's** (owner decision 4; R6 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-admin-focus.md`). A coach's home now shows their teams, **Your schedule** (their games and practices from today across every team they coach, the next ten with a count and links for the rest, TIME TBD entries with their reason) and **Your division** (every team of their divisions with its coaches' names, no contact details, from the new `get_my_division_teams`). **My Team** now opens the coach's team instead of sending them home as unauthorized. Coaches no longer reach the practice and game scheduling pages, scores, standings or practice lighting, and practice preferences stay. **Score entry and standings are league settings, off by default** (Settings, features): an admin turns each on, and until then the Scores and Standings pages are hidden. The server enforces it: migration `20261028010000` makes `update_game_score` admins and staff only, and only while `score_entry` is on (55000 otherwise; a coach could score their own team's games before), drops `request_practice_lighting_override` (no coach requests lighting; a request already stored stays for an admin to decide) and makes withdrawing a lighting window an admin's. Admins' **Preview as Coach** now appears: it looked for a profile role nothing sets. Permits for the fields a coach uses wait for Phase C. Production held no games, lighting rows or coach members; the revert (`docs/sql/20261028010000_revert.sql`) restores the coach arms. Smoke in `docs/sql/`, pgTAP in `supabase/tests/coach_view.sql`.
- **A parent who volunteers to coach no longer makes their child a coach; coaching leads are spread across teams** (Phase B6 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-b-teaming.md`). Before, the Teams page turned every player whose registration said a parent would coach into a coach of their own, so each of them anchored a team: a division of 88 with 13 such players got 13 teams. Now only a coach linked to their child (the coach import) anchors a team; a volunteering parent's child is a **coaching lead**. Teams are filled in the owner's order: coaches' children with their buddies; then leads, so no two teams differ by more than one lead (a buddy pair of two leads stays together); then experience, or rating when the league rates players, with a team that has fewer leads receiving more of the experienced players (one lead counts as one average player, tuned on the demo league). Whether a player is a lead comes from the player record, as the import stored it, so an answer of "Maybe" counts as it does on the Players page. A new league setting, **Spread Coaching Leads** (feature settings, on by default, audited), turns the spreading off; an import with no coaching column has no leads, and its teams are generated exactly as before. Team Builder's auto-balance follows the same rule and the league's settings instead of its own "Keep buddies together" and "Spread coach parents" toggles, and its audit entry records which settings it used. A lead is marked "Lead" beside the player's name in the roster editor and on the Team Builder board. Nothing about the volunteering parent is stored. A program's recommended team count no longer counts leads as head coaches. Re-runs place only late registrants by the same rule; nobody on a saved roster moves.
- **Team Builder finds buddies the way the Teams page does** (Phase B5 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-b-teaming.md`). Auto-balance and the "buddies met" count now match a buddy request through the same resolver as team generation (accents, case and punctuation ignored; first and last name, preferred and last name, or "Last, First"; two players of one name settled only when one names the requester back) and apply the league's **Buddy rule**. Under `Mutual requests only`, the default, only players who name each other are kept together and counted; a one-way request no longer pairs. Under `One-way requests, best effort`, a request whose named player listed no buddy is honoured where it costs no roster balance, never as a fixed pair, so many requests for one player cannot overfill that player's team. Buddy codes now pair on the board too, as on the Teams page, and a request or code naming a player in another division pairs nobody, including across the boys' and girls' divisions a co-ed board shows together.
- **Team counts per program, with warnings before and after generation** (Phase B4 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-b-teaming.md`). The Teams page's program overview now shows each program's recommended team count and a status from the same advice as the settings panel (for example "Teams over the maximum" or "Override not used"), instead of a "Ready" every program showed whether or not it could fit its roster limits. The advice counts the head coaches the import names, each of whom gets a team, so a program with more coaches than teams is told so before generation. Each program in the overview is a button, so it can be chosen from the keyboard. A run that did not record its limits says it was not checked, never that every team fits.
- **The Teams page stops on a failed run and lists who was not placed** (Phase B3 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-b-teaming.md`). A refused read of the coaches' team links, or a teaming step that fails, now stops generation with the error and stages nothing; before, the page went on with no coach anchors and, on a failure, no eligibility check. The staged or synced review gains **Players to review**: **Not placed** lists every player the run left off every team, with the reason in words (not eligible for the division, or no room) and their natural age, counted apart so a full team is never reported as eligibility; **Needs review** lists each player kept on a reviewed roster though not eligible, with their team and why; **Eligibility not checked** flags each placed player with no birthdate or age band to check against, beside their name (also on their card in Edit Mode), and holds nobody off a roster. A screen reader hears the lists once, when the review is staged. A program in which no player is eligible now says so and names them, instead of asking whether players were imported, and **Buddy requests** says why a pair is apart when one of them is not placed.
- **Team generation never seats a player whose play-up or play-down is blocked** (Phase B2 of `docs/changes/2026-10-22-phase-b-teaming.md`). The Teams page already classified each player's age against the division (a play-down, or a play-up with no coaching parent or qualifying buddy, is blocked), but the generator ignored it and seated them. A program in which every player is blocked stages no teams and stops with an error, instead of empty teams. Team ids, manual and locked assignments are unchanged, and a program with nobody blocked gets exactly the teams it got before. - **People data is read only by the people the access table admits** (Phase A3b of `docs/changes/2026-10-20-phase-a-privacy.md`).
