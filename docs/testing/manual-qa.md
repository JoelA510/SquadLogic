# Manual QA workbook

Use this workbook for the owner's Phase 1 sign-off. Record the deployment commit, date, browser, league time zone and season in your run notes. For each numbered workflow record **Pass**, **Fail** or **Not run**, the step reached and what you actually saw. A missing prerequisite gets a reason. A failed comparison gets the expected and actual counts, or the control and message involved. Keep receipts synthetic and omit calendar subscription links, credentials and contact data.

## Prepare two separate sessions

Use an admin account in a disposable QA organization on a test deployment for workflows 1–14. Use a second private browser window for the public demo in workflow 15. An enabled demo has a seeded league and its own import restrictions. Schedule exercises need an editable season with a past game date for score entry and future slots for practices. Keep season rollover until the end.

For registration and fields, use the synthetic [demo input files](../../fixtures/demo-2026). A small run can use a copy of one division's rows, with enough players for two teams. Check that the synthetic ages fit the division at the test season's age cutoff. Retain the registration IDs, choose two coaches and make a note of one team, one player, the venue and field label. Use the field file's header and adapt its dates to your test season. Provide two weekly practice slots on the same field and half at the same local time on Tuesday and Thursday, plus dated game slots. For the paired-day check, give both slots capacity for all the teams in the test division or use a division with one unheld team. Check that the test coach can attend both days. Reserve one team or division for a deliberate shortage. For schedule imports, copy a few rows of the [games file](../../fixtures/schedule-2026/schedule.csv) and the [practices file](../../fixtures/practice-schedule-2026/practice-schedule.csv). Replace the dates and labels in the copies with those of your QA season and teams, or run each complete fixture against its matching estate. Record baseline counts before each import.

## Workflows

1. **Enter the league and select the season.** Sign in as the QA admin. Check the organization and season in the header, open Players and then the workflow dashboard. Switch to a second available organization or season with a distinct synthetic player, then return. The list and dashboard should follow the selected context on each switch. From a signed-out private window try opening the admin pages directly. The sign-in screen should intervene. Keep that window for the demo.

2. **Import players, then coaches.** On Data Import, try a copy of the player file missing a required name header. Expect a validation message. Correct it and upload again. Inspect the preview, apply and follow the next step to Teams. Compare the division and player counts to the accepted rows. Inspect a player's natural age and division. In a coach CSV copy, add the optional `Child Registration ID` column. Give one coach a known player registration ID and another a synthetic ID that matches nobody. Open the coach import. Apply and read the link count and unmatched ID in the import log. Check Coaches. If testing rollback, use a separate small coach-only job, roll it back and check the coach list. The player count should stay the same. [Registration import contract](../architecture/gotsport-import-contract.md)

3. **Load facilities and availability.** Upload the field slot file, validate it for later review and visit **Fields & Venues** before applying. The new slots should first appear after Apply. Check venue, field, half, capacity, weekly practice times and dated game slots against the file. Reserve a practice slot to one division and check the reservation on reopening it. In **Fields & Venues**, find the Permits section and choose **Add permit**. Attach a synthetic facility permit and set its end date within the next 30 days. Open **Field Permits** to verify its field and end date. Check the expiry reminder on the admin home. Use a separate slot import for a rollback check so later workflows still have available fields.

4. **Import evaluations and form teams.** Enable the 1–5 player rating in the QA organization's features for this check. Choose Evaluations on Data Import. Use existing registration IDs, an unknown ID and a blank cell for a player who already has years played. Review the update and unmatched counts before applying. Confirm the rating on Players, that the old value survived the blank cell, and that the unknown ID added nobody. On Teams choose the division, inspect the recommended count, set roster limits and generate. Compare the rosters with the number eligible and read the explanation for each unplaced player. Check that a waitlisted player is excluded from the recommended count. Save the reviewed rosters and record two team names and their player IDs in private QA notes.

5. **Add a late registration.** Import a new eligible player for that division. From the dashboard use **Re-run Teaming**. Compare with step 4. The review should say how many saved teams it kept and how many late players it placed. Returning players should still be on their original teams. Check the new player appears once. Repeat with one too old for the available divisions. Look for the age reason under **Not placed** and verify the saved rosters still match the earlier copy.

6. **Check settings at all three levels.** Under Settings → General → Season & Calendar, open **Settings by program and division**. Use a named program with a division assigned to it. Set the organization value for counting outside games, override at the program and again at the division. Read the effective value and its source after each save and refresh. Choose **Inherit** at the division, then at the program, and watch the source change back. A division outside a named program inherits from the organization. In Settings → Features, check a supported switch and review its change in the settings audit log. Return temporary feature choices to their original values after testing dependent workflows.

7. **Set practice preferences and paired weekdays.** Under Practice preferences, open **Import a file**. Use a known coach email and matching team name, a weekday preference and a requested must. Include an unknown team. Preview should identify the unknown name and show how many musts await a decision. Import and review the pending musts. Accept one and verify the Rules tab. On Teams open **Division rules** beside the saved rosters, select the test division, set it to two practices a week and choose **Save Rules**. In the scoped settings from step 6 choose **Tue/Thu** at the division. Teams should show the effective pair and source. Change the count to one and check the setting says it does not apply. Return to two and confirm the saved choice reappears.

8. **Generate, review and apply practices.** With the matching Tue/Thu slots and compatible coach rules, open Practice Scheduling. Compare its capacity estimate with the team's two required practices. Run the auto-scheduler and inspect the proposed field, half and time on both days. Before applying, try **Mon/Wed** against a division offering only Tue/Thu. An unheld team should receive neither half of the impossible pair and the review should account for both missing practices. Restore Tue/Thu, rerun, apply and reload. Check the grid and the team's schedule. Lock a saved placement and rerun. Check the locked placement against its original day and slot. For an evening slot on an unlit field, open Practice daylight outlook and compare its warning with the saved time. If the venue lacks a usable sunset, expect an unknown-sunset warning. On a deployment with Heat Forecast enabled, select a game day and check the forecast time and any unavailable venues. Record an unavailable forecast as such.

9. **Import an existing practice schedule.** In a fresh division or season with matching teams and fields, prepare a practices file with a valid pair, a team on different weekdays from its configured pair and two rows claiming overlapping space. If using aliases, inspect the proposals against the [fixture alias file](../../fixtures/practice-schedule-2026/aliases.csv). Resolve names, preview, and read which conflicting rows can be saved, which are refused, and why. An off-pair assignment should be flagged and remain importable. Confirm the creations only after checking the counts. Inspect the saved, locked practices. Roll back this job and verify its additions disappear while practices saved before it remain.

10. **Walk a holiday week through repair.** Use two existing weekly practices on one field. Provide an alternate slot that fits only one of them. On **Blackout Dates**, preview a holiday-week closure, then save it. Open repair from the **saved blackout's row**. Review the recommended move and the TIME TBD reason for the other team. For a paired team compare the suggested change with its saved partner, including any pairing warning. If a partner was moved across a week boundary, check the dates on its team page and compare each practice in the proposed move’s week. Enact the recommendations and confirm the override when prompted. On each team page check the affected dates for **Practice (moved)** or **TIME TBD**. Check the week outside the blackout to confirm the original recurring series continues. Record the dates, decisions and visible reason privately for O1.

11. **Generate and protect games.** On Game Scheduling select a playable division, generate and save with one fewer usable slot than required. Account for scheduled games and each TIME TBD game with its reason. Check any practice-only division is identified separately. Publish one game or one division. Record a published matchup, date, time and field. In manual override inspect its move controls, regenerate and compare the recorded values. Choose **Allow changes**, give a listed reason, enter a synthetic note and tick the confirmation. Use the keyboard-accessible move control on an available slot, save and check the game history. Publish it again and verify its move controls are locked.

12. **Import and roll back games.** Work in a separate editable season or division. Include one outside opponent and a game without a time. Use the Games import. Map an existing team, explicitly identify the outside club, and read the preview's additions, clashes and unchecked items. Include a row that would move one saved, unpublished game and check that the preview counts one update. In a second Games tab, publish that game. Confirm the older import preview. Expect a “schedule changed” message, one fewer update and a fresh confirmation before anything is written. On the current preview tick the creation list, keep the publication baseline selected and confirm. Check the Published count and the dated TIME TBD row on Games. Reopen the import page and check its applied status. If the import created a division, give that division its own standings setting before rollback. Roll back, verify that the import's new games disappear, and read the message about the saved baseline. Check that a division with a saved scoped setting is reported as retained and still has that value. Separately stage and discard an unapplied job. It should disappear from the waiting list.

13. **Enter scores and inspect reports and exports.** In the QA organization enable **Score Entry** and **Standings** under Settings → Features. Open Scores & Results, enter 3–1 for a played game and reload. Inspect the standings for the win, points and goal difference. Compare a scored outside-club game under both values of the scoped setting from step 6. Turn both features off. Their navigation entries should disappear and their direct routes should return to the dashboard. Under Reports compare **Total Players** and **Active Teams** with the selected season. Use Teams & Rosters to cross-check player assignments for one saved team. Under Exports compare a **Day Sheet** with the same date on Games, filter its venue, and inspect its separate TIME TBD entries. Open **Coach schedule** for a coach with two teams and compare overlaps. In **Changes since publication**, choose the earlier baseline and compare a moved game with the old and new entries. In Output Generation choose **Generate CSVs**, then **Download Master CSV** and **Download Master XLSX**. Compare the scheduled teams and times in both files with the saved schedule. Read the notice for practices or games missing a time. On a team page download CSV, Excel and Calendar (.ics), and check the opponent, local times and recurring practices. Check the files have no contact columns. Open **Subscribe to Calendar**, test Copy and close the dialog without sharing its link.

14. **Start the next season.** Finish the editing workflows first. In Season & Calendar start a draft from the active season and set its age cutoff. Switch to the draft. Verify the programs and divisions were copied, import one renewed and one new synthetic registration, then compare Players in both seasons. The original season should still show its earlier roster. Activate the draft in this disposable organization. The previous season should become Completed, and its practice page should say it is read-only. Try its scoped program and division settings and verify they are read-only too.

15. **Try the public demo.** From the signed-out window choose **Try the demo** on a deployment where the owner has enabled it. Observe the preselected season, choose the other with the keyboard, then start. Check the header's season and countdown, a roster, and the dates under Season & Calendar. Try importing a file and check that the demo shows its account notice. Download the master schedule and open it to check it has practices. Choose **Leave demo**, verify sign-out, then start a fresh session for the other season. Record demo launch and expiry/purge checks in the [operator checklist](../operations/release-prep.md#demo-launch-owner-checklist).

## Owner sign-off

Run the keyboard steps with visible focus, and revisit the settings, import preview and repair panel in both themes. Record any unreadable text, clipped actions or lost focus against its workflow number. For a missing or disabled production feature, record the deployment and route without changing the production configuration during this workbook.
