## Claims

A claim is anything a reader could hold the product to: a number, a comparison, a guarantee, a
security or compliance status, a testimonial, or what an automated feature can do. Every claim
has a source someone could show a skeptic, an owner, and a review date. A row is added in the
same change that adds the claim. Security and compliance words ("secure", "compliant",
"certified", "encrypted") appear only when the control or certification exists and its owner
has signed off.
